Study Tracker widget

Open this page on your iPhone.

  1. Install Scriptable, a free app, from the App Store.
  2. Tap the button below to copy the widget.
  3. In Scriptable, tap +, paste, and rename the script Study Tracker at the top.
  4. Tap the play button and sign in with your Study Tracker email and password.
  5. On your Home Screen or Lock Screen, add a Scriptable widget. Tap the new widget while editing, and choose Study Tracker as the Script.
